# Editing Hubs [IN PROGRESS]

## Finding your Hub

To edit your hub, search for it in either Marva or directly in BFDB

In Marva, search for your hub in the Expression of [search for a Hub] field

![Expression of ( search for a Hub)](../images/image-1776353972108.png)

Select the Hub you'd like to edit and click on "view in BFDB"

![Select the hub you'd like to edit](../images/image-1776353845371.png)

In BFDB, use Text searching to find your Hub and make sure to select All Hubs under Work categories

![Searching for a Hub in BFDB](../images/image-1776354966896.png)
